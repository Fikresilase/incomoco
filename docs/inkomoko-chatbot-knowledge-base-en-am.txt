Chatbot answer policy
Answer in the language of the user; do not mix languages unless requested.
Use retrieved facts only. If no verified answer exists, acknowledge the gap and refer to an official Inkomoko contact.

Do not interpret regulatory rules as individual legal advice.
For financial decisions, request confirmation of current terms from Inkomoko.
Preserve document IDs and source-section metadata for retrieval and auditing.
INK-KB-EN-001 | What is Inkomoko?
Inkomoko is a social enterprise supporting entrepreneurs from displaced and host communities through financing, business training, advisory services, and market connections. It began in Rwanda in 2012 as African Entrepreneur Collective (AEC).
