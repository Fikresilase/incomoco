## Ethiopia overview

Inkomoko works in Ethiopia as part of its regional mission to support entrepreneurs in displacement-affected communities, including forcibly displaced entrepreneurs and host-community entrepreneurs.

Inkomoko's work in Ethiopia aligns with national reforms that support refugees' right to work and run businesses, including the Refugee Proclamation 1110/2024, the Right to Work Directive, and the Makatet/Inclusion Roadmap.

## Services Inkomoko provides

Inkomoko provides business support through three main pillars: Investment, Market Linkages, and Advocacy.

### Investment and access to finance

Inkomoko provides inclusive finance and personalized business support to entrepreneurs. This includes affordable loans and support designed to help entrepreneurs grow businesses, create jobs, sustain livelihoods, and build resilient local economies.

### Tailored learning and training

Inkomoko provides practical learning sessions for entrepreneurs. Training topics include financial literacy, business modeling, market access, and investment readiness.

### One-on-one advisory

Inkomoko provides personalized business advisory and coaching. Advisors help entrepreneurs refine business models, strengthen management, explore new markets, and address daily business challenges.

### Market linkages

Inkomoko helps entrepreneurs connect to wider markets and business ecosystems through networking, events, and private-sector engagement.

### Advocacy

Inkomoko works with partners and systems actors to support inclusive policies and market systems so forcibly displaced people and host communities can participate more fully in national economies.
